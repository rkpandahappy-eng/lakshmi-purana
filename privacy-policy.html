Privacy Policy – Lakshmi Purana (ଲକ୍ଷ୍ମୀ ପୁରାଣ)

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Lakshmi Purana ("the app") is made by Odi Techno Apps. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no sign-up, no account, no analytics and no advertising. It does not use the internet to read the Purana or show the puja guide.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings and progress (language, day / night mode, text size, reading position, bookmarks, sections read, ticked puja items, Gurubars marked done, and whether you accepted the disclaimer) are saved only on your own phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Reminders

If you turn on Gurubar reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone only; no data leaves the device.

Links and sharing

The app links to Odia Wikisource, the Creative Commons licence page and your email app. When you open them, those services' own privacy policies apply. If you use the Share button on a couplet, the text is passed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp).

Source of the text

The text of the Lakshmi Purana by Balarama Dasa is taken from Odia Wikisource and is available under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. The app is not linked to any temple, government or religious body.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
